I like the moment when a confusing system starts to make sense. That’s the thread running through my work — whether I’m exploring a web vulnerability in a lab, reading network traffic, supporting a user, or figuring out how a technology product should speak to people.

My technical practice includes controlled web security labs, network diagnostics, packet analysis, and scripting. I care about evidence, ethical boundaries, and documentation that another person can actually use.

And because technology doesn’t exist in a vacuum, I also work with marketing strategy and analytics. I’m interested in the bridge between how systems work and how people experience them.
